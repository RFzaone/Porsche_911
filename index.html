ORIGIN / EVOLUTION

One shape. Eight generations.

The 911 has changed almost everything beneath the surface while protecting the proportions that made it recognisable.

12 SEP 1963

The 901 debuts

Porsche presents its new 2+2 sports car at the Frankfurt motor show as the successor to the 356.

1964

The name becomes 911

The production model adopts the number that will define Porsche’s longest-running sports-car lineage.

1974

Turbo changes the formula

The 911 Turbo brings forced induction, a wider body and a new level of road-car performance.

1989

964 modernises the icon

All-wheel drive, ABS, power steering and more advanced electronics arrive without abandoning the silhouette.

1998

Water cooling arrives

The 996 generation makes the largest technical step yet with a water-cooled flat-six and a new platform.

2024

T-Hybrid enters production

The Carrera GTS adds an electric turbocharger, a PDK-integrated motor and a compact 400-volt battery.

SOFTWARE / DIGITAL ENGINEERING

The invisible half of the car.

Porsche does not publish its full proprietary codebase, but it publicly describes the interfaces, safety processes and validation methods surrounding modern vehicle software.

PORSCHE DIGITAL INTERACTION

The current 911 combines a configurable digital instrument cluster, Porsche Communication Management, connected services and vehicle-control software while keeping the driver at the centre.

01 / PCM

Communication Management

02 / INTERFACE

03 / CONNECTED

04 / SAFETY

Safety-related electronics are developed and verified with processes designed around automotive functional safety.
