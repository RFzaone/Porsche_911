PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED 911 DIGITAL STACK

PCM, connectivity and vehicle control.

Porsche describes the 911’s user-facing platform as Porsche Communication Management. It includes navigation, media, voice features, Porsche Connect services, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, native apps and online software/map updates.

Important accuracy note: Porsche has publicly confirmed Android Automotive OS for newer platforms such as the electric Macan. Its public 911 material names PCM but does not disclose the complete underlying 911 operating-system stack.

06 / LEGACY

Evolution, not replacement.

The 911 keeps its core silhouette and rear-engine identity while each generation rewrites the technology beneath it.

1963Original 911 revealed

The basic formula arrives: compact sports car, rear-mounted flat-six and a shape that becomes instantly recognisable.

2019992 generation

A wider, more digital 911 enters production while preserving the architecture and visual language of the original.

2021PCM 6.0

The 911 gains expanded connectivity, natural-language voice control, Android Auto and over-the-air update capability.

2024992.2 and T-Hybrid

The Carrera GTS introduces a performance-focused hybrid system and the 911 receives its first fully digital instrument cluster.

2025+Revised digital ecosystem

More powerful PCM hardware, Porsche App Center access, expanded Connect features and deeper digital integration arrive across the model range.
